# Portrait assets

The portfolio ships optimized WebP images. All portraits preserve Mohammad Awais’s identity from the supplied photographs.

| Asset | Placement | Source and treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hero-thoughtful.webp` | Main hero and aligned navbar overlap | User-supplied `Thoughtful Portrait with Sunglasses.png`; optimized with transparency preserved, without facial retouching or color grading. |
| `awais-cutout.webp` | Hero collage | Generated cutout based on `IMG20260919120135.jpg` and `IMG20260919120208.jpg`; transparent background and natural checked-shirt proportions. |
| `editorial-profile.webp` | Hero collage | Editorial edit based on `IMG20260919120208.jpg`, with `IMG_20260921_233332.jpg` as supporting identity reference. |
| `about-editorial.webp` | Hero collage and subtle background | Folded-arms portrait edited from `IMG20260921152836.jpg`, with a neutral studio background. |
| `about-personal.webp` | About section | Edited from `IMG_20260921_190623.jpg`; balanced natural lighting and a softly textured neutral background. |

Edited portraits were produced with the built-in image generation tool. Treatments preserve facial proportions, realistic skin texture, clothing, and recognizable likeness. The supplied design showcase was used only as a composition reference.

Only display assets are included. Superseded PNG masters and rejected portrait variants are excluded. The hero’s two portrait layers use the same image and geometry to prevent seams; the bottom fade is applied in CSS.
